From download to playback

  1. Download the ZIP and extract the entire StreamLens folder.
  2. Double-click Start-StreamLens.cmd. Your browser opens the local tool.
  3. Paste a streaming page URL and click Analyze.
  4. Use Preview or copy the master link. Close the local service with Stop-StreamLens.cmd.

This page describes and distributes the desktop tool. Analysis runs on your computer. Internet is required for broadcaster pages and live streams. Authentication, DRM and website restrictions can prevent extraction; new domains require an allowlist update.
